There is an island

C'è un'isola unica

Within sight of Dubai, yet a world apart. The first Maserati residences, moored off Dubai: like living on a private yacht, facing the city's skyline, with the sea on every side. Cast off from the mainland and, about twenty minutes later, you are home.

Maserati Island is where you ease off the throttle, and the engine settles into a soothing purr. Here, you revitalise and recharge. You laugh more, and breathe easier. Leggerezza. Luminosità. Vitalità.

The World Island

How do people live on an island? A man-made world off Dubai's coast, and no longer a dream: it is being built now.

What is The World Islands?

A man-made group of islands off Dubai's coast, shaped like a map of the world.

300Man-made islands

~4 kmOff the Jumeirah coast, facing the skyline
100 mAverage distance between islands
232 kmOf new shoreline created
Boat onlyNo road connects the islands to the city

The Breakwater

An oval wall of rocks and concrete blocks around all the islands. Maintained by Nakheel, not by the buyer.

232 kmTotal length
26 kmOval wall
  • Stops big waves — waves from the open sea hit the wall first and break there.
  • Protects the beaches — keeps the sand and beaches from washing away.
  • Calmer water inside — smoother and safer boat rides, even in rough weather.

How life works on an island?

Everything a home in the city has, made and managed on the island itself.

    Frequently asked questions

    01The World was announced 20+ years ago. Why will it work now?

    It is no longer a vision: hotels operate, several developers are building at once, and Amali and Zuha villas have registered at AED 68–220 million. One&Only and Ritz-Carlton have committed.

    02Why not simply buy on Palm Jumeirah?

    Compare like with like. Palm super-branded homes trade at AED 7,000–8,500 per sq ft and The Heart of Europe at 5,000–6,500. Maserati Island launches below both, with a global brand and a private island.

    03What if the neighbouring islands stay undeveloped?

    Value is anchored inside our island: SAMANA builds the utilities and runs the boats, Nakheel maintains the breakwater. Every island that develops adds amenities; every one that doesn't keeps supply scarce.

    04Will it feel like a real community?

    Yes. More than 80 amenities, an island market, pharmacy, clinic and banking point, plus residents' lounge and events. Belonging is built in through the Owners Club.

    05There is no road. Why buy a home reached only by boat?

    Controlled access is the luxury: no through-traffic and no tourists. Residents board from several mainland marinas and are home in 15 to 25 minutes, about 30 door to door.

    06How often do the boats run?

    Two boats every 30 minutes until 8 pm, then one boat until midnight, with an emergency boat on demand.

    07How do deliveries and groceries work?

    An island market with a convenience store, a parcel room and laundry valet. Deliveries go to mainland security, then come over by boat.

    08What about healthcare and schools?

    An island clinic with a paramedic, a pet clinic and an emergency boat on demand. Schools in Jumeirah are about 20 minutes away.

    09Why trust SAMANA with an offshore project?

    55+ projects launched, top five by off-plan sales volume, 1,300+ units handed over since 2017. Every payment sits in a RERA-regulated escrow account, released against certified progress.

    10Who provides power, water and utilities?

    SAMANA builds all island utilities to DEWA and authority standards, with backup power, water and communications. No completion certificate is issued without them.

    11Is Maserati the developer?

    No. SAMANA is the developer. Maserati's involvement centres on the interior design and the supply of certain Maserati products. Refer contract questions on the brand agreement to management.

    12What do owners get beyond the home?

    Membership of the Maserati Island Owners Club UAE: scenic drives, social events, weekend getaways, brand storytelling and VIP passes.

    13Can the price change if construction costs rise?

    No. The SPA price is fixed under Dubai's off-plan framework (Law 13/2008). Cost inflation is the developer's risk, not the buyer's.

    14What return can you promise?

    None, and never promise one. Show the register instead: Dubai prime homes averaged AED 3,767 per sq ft in Q3 2025, up 8.4% year on year (Knight Frank).

    15Who will rent these homes?

    Today, the resort-style short-stay guest who already fills island and Palm rentals; later, residents commuting to DIFC. Present it as a hospitality yield, not a long-lease yield.

    16Can I resell before handover?

    Yes, by assigning the SPA: a developer NOC, then an Oqood transfer at a DLD trustee office, typically once 30% is paid. Position it as a five- to ten-year hold.
